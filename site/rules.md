### General

- No tanking or rebuild-tanking for draft picks. All owners are expected to compete each week.
- Trade vetoes require a majority (2 of 3 owners) using the decision log. Sleeper veto is disabled.

### Keeper

- Keeper cost is the round directly above where the player was originally drafted (e.g., a 4th-round pick costs a 3rd).
- Undrafted free-agent acquisitions kept as keepers cost a 5th-round pick.
